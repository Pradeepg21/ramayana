Ramayana – The Journey of Shri Ram

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

In short: Ramayana does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no accounts, no in‑app purchases, no analytics and no internet access.

About this app

Ramayana – The Journey of Shri Ram (“the app”, package com.satya.ramayana) is a story game for children that retells the Ramayana through ten illustrated stages. This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to every version of the app on Google Play.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or device information. The app:

  • does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, photo, location or any other personal detail;
  • does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything off the device;
  • contains no advertising, analytics, crash‑reporting or tracking software, and no third‑party SDKs that collect data;
  • does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location;
  • has no chat, social features or links that take a child out of the app.

Information stored on the device

To let a child continue where they left off, the app saves a small amount of game progress only on the device: which stages and levels are unlocked, stars and best scores, and the sound and voice settings. This information never leaves the app, is not linked to any person, and is deleted when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. Starting a “New Journey” in the app also clears it.

If the device owner has turned on Android’s own backup service, Android may include this game progress in the device backup to the owner’s Google account. That backup is managed by Google under the owner’s settings, not by us, and we cannot access it.

Story narration

The app reads stories aloud using the text‑to‑speech engine already installed on the device (for example, Google’s Speech Services). Only the app’s own story text is passed to that engine; no personal information is involved. Narration can be turned off at any time with the “Voice” button.

Children’s privacy

The app is designed for children and families. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete. The app follows Google Play’s Families Policy and is designed to be consistent with children’s privacy laws such as COPPA (USA), GDPR‑K (EU/UK) and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Sharing of information

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Security

Game progress is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. Any change that would involve collecting information from children would require verifiable parental consent first.
