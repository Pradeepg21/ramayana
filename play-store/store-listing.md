## Full description  (4000 characters max)

```
Bring the timeless story of the Ramayana to life! Ramayana: Journey of Shri Ram is a gentle, colourful story game for children. They follow Shri Ram from his childhood in Ayodhya to the joyful return home on Diwali, learning the epic one adventure at a time.

★ THE WHOLE EPIC IN 10 STAGES
Every stage is a scene from the Ramayana with its own simple tap-and-play game:
• Bal Ram at the Gurukul – learn archery with Guru Vashishtha
• Protect the Yagna – guard Rishi Vishwamitra's sacred fire from flying demons
• Sita Swayamvar – lift and string Lord Shiva's mighty bow
• Vanvas – run and jump through the forest to Panchavati
• The Search for Sita – catch the jewels Sita dropped from the Pushpak Viman
• Meeting Hanuman – tap lotuses carrying Ram's name and earn Hanuman's special power
• Help Sugriv – stand by the vanar prince in his duel with mighty Bali
• Build the Vanar Sena – gather a great army of vanars and bears
• Ram Setu – write Ram's name on stones so they float and form the bridge
• The Final Battle – break Ravan's pride as good wins over evil in Lanka

★ 30 LEVELS THAT GROW WITH YOUR CHILD
Finish all 10 stages to unlock Level 2, then replay the whole journey a little faster with bigger goals. Complete Level 2 to unlock Level 3, the ultimate challenge! Young players build skill and confidence step by step. Collect up to 3 stars in every stage of every level – 90 stars in all!

★ STORIES READ ALOUD
A warm Indian-English narrator reads every story page, fact and lesson aloud, so even children who are just learning to read can enjoy the journey. Narration can be switched on or off at any time.

★ LEARN SOMETHING NEW IN EVERY LEVEL
• Story pages tell the Ramayana in simple words
• "Did you know?" pages share 30 fascinating facts – Kevat the boatman, Bharat and Ram's paduka, brave Jatayu, Shabari's berries, the little squirrel at Ram Setu, why we celebrate Dussehra and Diwali, and more
• Each level ends with a value to remember – kindness, truth, courage, teamwork and respect for elders
• "Remember what we learnt" pages connect each lesson to the next

★ MADE FOR CHILDREN
• Gentle and non-violent: nobody is ever shown being hurt – when time runs out, it is simply "Time up!" and you try again
• Beautiful hand-styled art, festive music, diyas, rangoli and Diwali fireworks
• Celebrate the wedding of Ram and Sita and the grand Diwali homecoming to Ayodhya
• Progress is saved automatically – continue right where you left off

★ SAFE AND PRIVATE
• No ads
• No in-app purchases
• No internet needed – play anywhere, even offline
• No accounts, no data collection, no tracking

A wonderful way for children aged 5 and up, parents and grandparents to share the stories, festivals and values of the Ramayana together.

Jai Shri Ram!
```
